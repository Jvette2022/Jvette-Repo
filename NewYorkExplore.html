Itineraries

Choose a length. Each day groups stops that sit near each other, so you spend time looking, not riding.

What to eat

The best meals in New York are often cheap, fast, and eaten standing up.

Pizza by the slice

A plain cheese slice from a corner shop runs a few dollars. Fold it lengthwise, the way locals do.

Bagels with lox

Order an everything bagel with cream cheese and smoked salmon from a neighborhood bagel shop, early.

Halal cart platters

Chicken over rice with white sauce, from a street cart in Midtown. Filling, quick, and inexpensive.

Dim sum in Chinatown

Go with a group so you can order many small plates, then wander Mott Street afterward.

Deli pastrami

A towering hot pastrami on rye at a Lower East Side deli. Share one; they are huge.

Dollar-slice energy, any hour

Many places stay open late. Hungry at midnight is a New York problem with easy answers.

Getting around

You do not need a car. You will want comfortable shoes.

Tap to pay on the subway

The subway and buses accept contactless cards and phones at the turnstile. No need to buy a separate card.

Check uptown or downtown

Subway entrances are labeled by direction. Confirm which way your train goes before you swipe in.

Walk the grid

In Manhattan above Houston Street, avenues run north–south and streets run east–west. Twenty blocks is about a mile.

Use the ferry

The Staten Island Ferry is free and passes the Statue of Liberty. It is the cheapest harbor cruise there is.

Airports

JFK and Newark connect to the city by rail. LaGuardia is best reached by bus or taxi. Leave extra time.

Stay in your lane

On crowded sidewalks, walk steadily and step aside before you stop to take photos or check a map.

When to go

Every season works. They just feel different.

Spring

Mild days and cherry blossoms in Central Park and the Brooklyn Botanic Garden. Pack layers for changing weather.

Summer

Hot and humid, with free outdoor concerts, rooftop bars, and ferries to the beach. Book hotels early.

Fall

Crisp air and bright foliage in the parks. Often the most comfortable season for walking all day.

Winter

Cold, with holiday windows, ice skating, and cozy museums. Fewer crowds in January and February.
