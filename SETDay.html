About SET Days

SET Days is the yearly celebration of the School of Engineering and Technology at NU Fairview. It is a time when students, teachers, and guests come together to show what they have learned and built.

This year's theme is "Code the Future." Over three days, students can join hands-on workshops, friendly competitions, project showcases, and talks from people working in the technology industry.

The event is meant to inspire creativity and teamwork. Whether you are a first-year student just starting out or a graduating student about to enter the industry, there is something for you.

Event Dates: December 2–4, 2026

Purpose: To promote learning, innovation, and school spirit in engineering and technology.

Who can join: All NU Fairview students, faculty, alumni, and invited guests.

  4. Wait for your confirmation message.
  5. Show up at the venue 15 minutes early with your school ID.

Event and Technology Terms

Hackathon
A timed event where teams build a working project, like an app or a website.
Workshop
A hands-on session where participants learn a skill by doing it.
Robotics
The field of designing and building machines that can move and do tasks.
Capstone
A final project that students complete to show what they learned in their course.

Participation Guidelines

Follow these steps to take part in SET Days:

  1. Read the activity list and pick the ones you like.
  2. Register online before the deadline.
  3. Form a team if the activity needs one (for example, the hackathon).
  4. Wait for the confirmation message from the organizers.
  5. Come to the venue on time and bring your school ID.
